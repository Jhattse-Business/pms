**What to look for:**
- Real-time synchronization across all booking channels
- Two-way integration with major OTAs
- Centralized reservation dashboard
- Automatic inventory updates
- Channel performance analytics

### 3. Integrated Billing and GST Compliance

**Why it matters:** Indian hotels must comply with complex GST regulations. Incorrect tax calculations can lead to penalties during audits. Your **billing software in India** must handle multiple tax slabs, exemptions, and generate compliant invoices.

**What to look for:**
- Automatic GST calculation (5%, 12%, 18%, 28% as applicable)
- GST-compliant invoice generation
- Support for multiple tax heads (CGST, SGST, IGST)
- HSN code integration
- Tax reports for filing returns
- Handling of exempted services

**Jhattse Advantage:** Jhattse Business Software takes the stress out of GST compliance. Our **billing software in India** automatically calculates the correct tax based on room tariffs and services, generates GST-compliant invoices, and maintains detailed records for audits. Stay compliant effortlessly.
