## 🌟 Key Features

### 1. 🖥️ Hotel Management System (PMS) – Front Desk
Efficiently manage your entire property from one screen.
- **Fast Check-in/Check-out:** Streamline guest arrival and departure
- **Document Management:** Upload and store guest IDs (Aadhaar, PAN) digitally
- **Interactive Dashboard:** View real-time occupancy, room status, and daily revenue

### 2. 🔄 Channel Manager (Real-Time Sync)
Maximize your online visibility without the fear of double bookings.
- **OTA Integration:** Sync rates and inventory in real-time across Booking.com, Agoda, MakeMyTrip, and Airbnb
- **Centralized Inventory:** Update room availability once, and it reflects everywhere instantly
- **Prevent Overbooking:** Real-time sync eliminates double bookings

### 3. 🍽️ Restaurant POS & Room Service
Manage your hotel's dining and room service effortlessly.
- **Unified Billing:** Add food orders directly to the guest's room bill
- **QR Code Ordering:** Allow guests to scan a QR code to order food digitally
- **KOT Management:** Streamline kitchen operations with digital Kitchen Order Tickets

### 4. 💬 WhatsApp Business Integration
Engage with guests where they are most active.
- **Automated Updates:** Send booking confirmations, welcome messages, and invoices via WhatsApp
- **Direct Interaction:** Solve guest queries instantly without phone calls

### 5. 🧹 Smart Housekeeping Management
Keep your rooms ready for the next guest with automated workflows.
- **Auto-Task Creation:** Rooms automatically marked "Dirty" upon checkout
- **Staff Coordination:** Assign tasks to specific staff members and track completion in real-time

### 6. 📅 Booking Engine (Direct Reservations)
Save on OTA commissions by driving direct bookings.
- **Commission-Free:** Accept bookings directly from your website or social media
- **Mobile Optimized:** Seamless booking experience on any device

### 7. 💳 Payment Gateway Integration
Secure and flexible payment options.
- **Online Payments:** Collect advance payments via Credit Card, Debit Card, UPI, or Net Banking
- **Payment Records:** Track partial payments, advances, and pending dues easily
