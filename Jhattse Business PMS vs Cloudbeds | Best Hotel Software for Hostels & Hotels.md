## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Cloudbeds better for hostels than Jhattse Business?
Cloudbeds has strong hostel-specific features like bed-level inventory management. However, **Jhattse Business** also supports hostel operations with room/bed configurations and is more cost-effective for Indian hostels. For hostels targeting international backpackers, Cloudbeds may have an edge. For domestic travelers, Jhattse is superior.

### Can I connect Indian OTAs with Cloudbeds?
Cloudbeds has limited direct connectivity with Indian OTAs like MakeMyTrip and Goibibo. You may need channel manager partners or manual updates. **Jhattse Business** provides direct two-way integration with all major Indian OTAs.

Yes! Jhattse Business offers multi-property dashboards allowing you to manage multiple hotels, resorts, or hostels from a single login with consolidated reporting and centralized rate management.

- Passport scanning and foreigner registration (C-Form)

Very easy! Our migration team handles:
