Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1: What is hotel management software, and how does it help Indian hotels?

Hotel management software (PMS) automates daily operations like reservations, check-in/check-out, billing, housekeeping, and reporting. For Indian hotels, it ensures GST compliance, integrates with local OTAs (MakeMyTrip, Goibibo), supports Indian payment methods (UPI, Paytm), and manages seasonal pricing during festivals and holidays. Jhattse Business PMS reduces manual work by 60% and increases direct bookings through our built-in booking engine.

Q2: Is your restaurant POS software suitable for small cafes and cloud kitchens?

Absolutely! Our restaurant POS software scales from small cafes to multi-outlet chains. Cloud kitchens benefit from Zomato/Swiggy integration that automatically syncs orders. Small cafes love our affordable monthly plans with no setup fees. Features include QR code ordering, table management, inventory tracking, and GST billing—all designed for Indian F&B businesses.

Q3: Do you provide channel manager integration with Booking.com and other OTAs?

Yes! Our channel manager connects with 50+ OTAs including Booking.com, Agoda, Expedia, MakeMyTrip, Goibibo, Yatra, and Airbnb. Real-time synchronization prevents overbooking, automatically updates rates and availability, and consolidates all reservations in one dashboard. This increases your online visibility while saving commission costs through direct booking incentives.

Q4: Can I access the software from multiple locations or on my mobile?

Yes! Jhattse Business is cloud-based, so you can access it from any device with internet—laptop, tablet, or smartphone. Our mobile apps (Android & iOS) let you monitor occupancy, approve bookings, check reports, and manage staff remotely. Multi-property owners can switch between locations instantly. Data is backed up securely on Indian servers.

Q5: Is the software GST compliant for Indian businesses?

100% GST compliant! Our software automatically calculates CGST, SGST, and IGST based on your location and customer state. Generates GST-compliant invoices with HSN/SAC codes, files GSTR-1 reports, and maintains audit trails. Regular updates ensure compliance with latest GST rules. Trusted by 500+ Indian hotels and restaurants for accurate tax filing.
