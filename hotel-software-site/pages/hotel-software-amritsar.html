Amritsar's Thriving Hospitality Industry

Pilgrimage & Heritage Tourism Hub

Amritsar receives over 10 million pilgrims and tourists annually, with peak seasons during Vaisakhi (April), Diwali, and Gurpurabs. The Golden Temple complex alone welcomes 100,000+ visitors daily. The city's hospitality industry has grown significantly with improved connectivity via Sri Guru Ram Dass Jee International Airport and the upcoming Amritsar-Kolkata Industrial Corridor.

Border Tourism & Business Travel

The Wagah Border ceremony attracts thousands daily, creating demand for same-day visits and overnight stays. Additionally, Amritsar's position as a trading hub near the Pakistan border generates consistent business travel, requiring hotels to cater to both leisure pilgrims and corporate guests with different needs.

Unique Operational Challenges

Hotels in Amritsar face distinct challenges: managing massive influx during religious festivals, coordinating with tour operators for Golden Temple visits, handling large family groups, providing specialized vegetarian/Punjabi cuisine, managing early morning and late-night check-ins for pilgrims, and maintaining high turnover rates during peak seasons efficiently.
