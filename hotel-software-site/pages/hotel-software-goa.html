Is Jhattse Business PMS suitable for vacation rentals and villas?

Absolutely! Our software includes dedicated features for vacation rentals including multi-unit management, automated messaging for self-check-in, coordinating cleaning services across scattered properties, and integration with Airbnb and Vrbo.

Can the system handle seasonal pricing variations?

Yes! Goa's extreme seasonal variations are built into our pricing engine. Set different rate calendars for peak season, monsoon discounts, festival surcharges, and long-stay offers. The system automatically applies correct rates based on booking dates.
